Great capture of this excellent show! The first 30-ish minutes have obstruction on the bottom left of the stage and sometimes further center when they lean to the side; some action is missed because of this but it’s worked around as best as possible. The rest is mostly unobstructed. Otherwise shot very well in a great mix of close-ups, mediums, and wide-shots. Some wandering, shaking, and re-adjusting throughout. A couple of brief dropouts. Audio is patched in from an external source; my audio recorder scrapes up against my bag a LOT in this recording and it is often audible. Some grain when the show gets very dark. Overall a great capture.
